Before the First Date

Practice dating before the emotional stakes are real.

A one-month, fully virtual coaching program with Nicole and Casey. Get clear on what you want in a partner, practice the early conversations with a coach playing the match, and choose who you date more carefully.

Fully virtual. Two trauma-informed coaches. You can pause any conversation at any moment.

Book a free fit call

Who it's for

This is for you if:

  • You've done personal growth work, like therapy, coaching, or programs, and you're ready to use it in dating.
  • You understand why you've picked the wrong person before, but you haven't had a chance to practice picking the right one.
  • Dating apps feel like a lot of effort for very little clarity, or you're not on them yet and want to be ready first.
  • You'd rather practice a hard conversation than have it for the first time with someone you like.

What you'll practice

Therapy gives you the tools. Most people get almost no practice using them before the stakes are real.

In one month, you build:

Discernment

Recognizing compatibility, healthy interest, warning signs, and your own attraction patterns.

Communication

Expressing interest, needs, boundaries, and preferences without abandoning yourself.

Emotional steadiness

Noticing when you're activated and choosing a response instead of reacting.

How it works

Fully virtual, with messaging between our video calls.

  1. 20 to 30 minutesFree fit callWe talk about your goals and whether this is the right fit.
  2. 75 minutesCall 1Your history, your patterns, three situations that usually activate you, and two personal practice goals.
  3. Weeks 1 and 2Several matchesCasey texts you in character as several different potential matches, one at a time, with a short debrief between each.
  4. 45 minutesCall 2A midpoint review.
  5. Weeks 3 and 4One promising matchSomeone compatible with you, not perfect, building toward a video date.
  6. 60 minutesCall 3A practice video date with Casey, with Nicole present as your ally, then a review and a plan for real dating.

Two text threads run all month. In the practice thread, Casey is in character and Nicole observes. In the coaching thread, all three of us step out of character to reflect, answer your questions, and give you both a man's and a woman's perspective.

Already dating? Bring your real matches and conversations into coaching too. Not on apps yet? All practice happens in simulation, so you don't need to be.

Safe by design

You're in control the whole time.

  • Everything is virtual: text and video only.
  • You always know when Casey is in character. Practice never happens on real dating apps or with real people.
  • Scenarios are agreed in advance. Nothing distressing is sprung on you.

Pause. Resume. End.

Say "Pause" to stop and talk it through, "Resume" to go back in, or "End" to finish, no explanation needed.

What you leave with

  • Your Healthy Dating Blueprint: your patterns and triggers, the compatibility signs that matter to you, the practices that worked, your boundaries, and your next steps.
  • A before-and-after look at the three situations that used to activate you.
