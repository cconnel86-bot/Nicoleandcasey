Youniverse 2.0 Origins

Meet the team creating your experience.

“They say that what doesn’t kill you makes you stronger, as if going through something hard is all it takes. That’s like saying that all you have to do to be a good fighter is step into the ring with a champion. The reason the traumas I endured made me stronger is because I was fortunate to already have the practices I needed before they happened. Then with each one I became more and more resilient - supporting multiple family members in my 20s on a teacher’s salary, losing my home to Hurricane Sandy, a home birth gone wrong, the death of my brother, divorce, the financial fallout from a failed business, living as a single mom - until I was able to deal with the COVID pandemic as if it were just another day. Therapy is a lifesaver but we also need practices to support our brain, hormones, and immune system when we’re being pushed beyond our limit. We can use the same practices and skills when we choose to push our limits in order to pursue goals and create a life we love.”
Nicole Cruz
Host

Nicole Cruz is a Holistic Wellness and Life Coach. She works with those who are forging their own path outside of the mainstream, whether by choice or due to circumstances beyond their control. Her clients are often neurodivergent, entrepreneurs, or facing difficult circumstances.

Nicole merged her backgrounds in holistic health, fitness, and education into a unique practice. Her methods optimize the mind and body to help her clients stay strong and centered as they navigate the most challenging circumstances. She guides them to improve their skills and relationships to forge a path to the life they desire - one that aligns with their values and who they are.

As a consultant for heart led business owners, she earned the nickname “the Marie Kondo of the mind.” She helps her clients to spend more time and energy in their zone of genius and less on everything that drains them by simplifying, systematizing, and delegating.

“As a divorced father of three boys, this work is personal to me. It brings me joy to help others along their paths, too. I lost my mom almost 5 years ago and that, along with a few other events, were catalysts in the process of evolving into the best version of myself. The pain I went through over that period of time was bad, but was also just what I needed to make sure that my boys wouldn’t have to endure the same hardships I have. My life goal became shifting the world slowly into one they can thrive in; giving them and the rest of society the tools we all need to repair and heal from abuses, traumas, and other misalignments in whatever forms that takes; and planting as many healthy seeds as I can along the way.”
Casey Connell
Host

Casey Connell is a Nationally Certified Divorce Mediator and Personal Finance Coach with a background in Engineering and Science with a national laboratory. His skillset is derived from a deep appreciation for the complexity of human behavior and how complex systems work.

Professionally he has always focused on efficiency and innovation. He finds innovative ways to reach goals faster. He easily combines new methods and ideas that challenge the status quo, with compassion and a desire to understand. His work has led him to appreciate that the best opportunities in life come from a collision of knowledge and skills. Five years ago he began a journey to shift his focus to personal growth. He met and worked with some amazing professional coaches and created relationships that shifted his entire view of the world.

In this phase of his life he’s creating spaces for others to share in a similar experience, one that is dedicated to growth however is genuine to them. He creates spaces where people feel comfortable in their own skin, seen, and where they feel able to dive into the essence of their own being to evolve into the next best version of themselves.

Anita Ruderman
Yoga Instructor

Anita Ruderman, RYT was certified in 1992 and has been teaching the Hot26 Series and Vinyasa Yoga continuously since then.

As owner of Hot Yoga Rockaway Beach in Rockaway Park, NY for more than 14 years, she nourishes all of her passions; connecting with students, supporting the community and providing a safe and nurturing environment for “every body” to practice yoga.

She has a dynamic personality and is an inspiring and motivating teacher. With over 30 years of experience, Anita brings compassion, humor and a unique wit and charm to her classes, stressing that yoga is a “practice not a perfect” which puts newcomers at ease. Students respond to her magic and leave her class feeling balanced and whole. She is easily accessible and happily answers questions about a pose, modifications, benefits, etc…and she can’t wait to see you sweat in the hot room!

ADD PHOTO — SUSAN
Susan Sachs
Licensed Acupuncturist

With a commitment to helping others, Dr. Susan Sachs has been a licensed acupuncturist and practitioner of integrative medicine since 1998.

In addition to private practice, she shares her passion for this medicine as a clinical supervisor at Pacific College of Oriental Medicine in New York City, and has taught on the Masters degree level for several years. In 2001, she created the acupuncture program at the Joseph P. Addabbo Family Health Center, and has been a clinical supervisor at Columbia University, and for World Cares, (an outreach program for those affected by 911 and Hurricane Katrina).

For over 15 years she has helped couples to conceive naturally and as an adjunct to assisted reproductive protocols. Additionally she treats endocrine, allergy, musculoskeletal and pain issues through the powerful tools of acupuncture, Chinese herbology, and Endobiogeny (a French originated systems based medicine that uses lab analysis as part of its diagnostics and European herbal protocols to treat a wide variety of issues).

She trained in Classical Chinese Medicine under the guidance of Chinese Master Jeffrey Yuen, and has worked closely with Dr. Yan Wu, author, teacher, and specialist in fertility using Chinese Medicine. She is also trained in NAET, an integrative technique for eliminating allergies.
